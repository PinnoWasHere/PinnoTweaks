# PinnoTweaks

A [Daylight UI](https://github.com/dayightt/DaylightUI) plugin for WoW Forever. It adds more
ways to style Daylight UI's bars and action buttons, drawn with Daylight UI's own look.

Everything is off when you install it. Open `/dui` and find the PinnoTweaks section.

## What it does

- **Bars:** gradient direction, diagonal and radial fills, fill opacity, pastel and dark
  fills, background colors, a ghost bar for health just lost, a low health color, a spark on
  the leading edge, and heal and absorb prediction with its own colors, styles and glow.
- **Action bars:** mana costs on buttons, with full text controls.

Every frame can follow the shared defaults page or keep its own settings.

## Needs

- WoW Forever
- Daylight UI 2.4.1 or newer
