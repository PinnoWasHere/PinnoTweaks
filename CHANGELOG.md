# PinnoTweaks

## 0.1.0

The first release. Everything is off until you turn it on, so installing changes nothing.

### Bars

Unit frames (player, target, focus, pet, party, raid, arena), cast bars, swing timers and
nameplates. Settings are under /dui, in the PinnoTweaks section. Frame defaults sets the look
for every frame, and each frame's tab can keep its own values instead.

- Gradient direction for health, power and cast bars, with a flip
- Diagonal and radial fill textures
- Fill opacity, pastel and dark fills, and a background colored by value, class or death
- A ghost bar that shows the health just lost, then fades
- A low health color below a cutoff you set
- A spark on the bar's leading edge, hidden at full
- Heal and absorb prediction: your heals and others' heals in their own colors, three absorb
  styles, heal absorbs, and a glow when a shield overflows the bar
- Swing timer bars take the same looks

### Action bars

- Mana costs on action buttons, macros included. Font, size, outline, color, position on a
  nine-spot grid, nudges, opacity, short numbers, and red when you lack the mana.
- Button defaults sets the text look that the mana cost follows unless you give it its own.
