# PinnoTweaks

## 0.1.1

Fixes for low health, the spark and the ghost.

- Two-shade low health no longer paints every frame red. It shows only below your cutoff,
  on your own frames and on Daylight UI's sample frames alike.
- Low health now sits over the bar's fill texture and Light, under the frame's text.
- Sample frames (test mode, the options preview) show low health and the spark from the
  health they show, and follow it as it changes.
- In test mode, the target, focus, pet and arena frames show every bar tweak from their
  sample, not from your real target, focus and pet.
- The fill opacity now fades the bar of an enemy player whose class is hidden, in Class
  gradient too.
